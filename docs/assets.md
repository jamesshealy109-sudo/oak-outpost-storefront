## Landscape

`public/images/long-way-home.png` was generated using the built-in image generation tool on October 26, 2026. The exact prompt is saved in [hero-image-prompt.txt](hero-image-prompt.txt) and embedded in the PNG. It is editorial brand atmosphere, not an actual location or product claim. The subject is a Southern backroad under live oaks and Spanish moss, with a distant pickup. No people or merchandise were fabricated as product evidence.

## Product photographs

Actual imagery is supplied dynamically by the Fourthwall Storefront API for the owner's products. The site preserves variant image relationships. No generated apparel mockups replace those photographs.

## Fonts

Fraunces SemiBold and DM Sans Regular/SemiBold are self-hosted in `public/fonts/`. They were downloaded from Google Fonts' `fonts.gstatic.com` distribution. Their SIL Open Font License files are included as `Fraunces-LICENSE.txt` and `DM-Sans-LICENSE.txt`, from the [Fraunces](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces) and [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans) font directories.

## Icons

Lucide React supplies the interface icons under its bundled ISC license.
