# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user after approving the proposed scope: Next.js with TypeScript. The builder selects the remaining design details.

## Users

People shopping for Oak & Outpost Southern lifestyle and outdoor apparel, on phones and computers. The owner manages merchandise and orders through Fourthwall initially.

## Product Purpose

A standalone, substantially redesigned brand website and functional shop in a new private GitHub repository. Success means visitors can discover real products, choose valid variants, manage their cart, and reach the matching checkout.

## Operating Context

Fourthwall provides payments, print-on-demand fulfillment, shipping, and order management for the first few months. The owner plans to switch to dropshipping later; that supplier is not yet chosen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Home, shop, product detail, cart, about, contact, and FAQ. Live data comes from Fourthwall. Private products are not available in the public Storefront API; prices and publication must be settled for launch. Future fulfillment is an adapter boundary, not an invented supplier integration. Payment information is entered only at the provider checkout.

## Brand Commitments

Oak & Outpost LLC. “Made for the Long Way Home.” Southern Lifestyle Goods, Est. 2026. Forest green, cream, tan, and burnt orange. Rustic, outdoorsy, vintage, and clean. Preserve supplied artwork and actual product descriptions.

## Product Principles

- Real products, honest availability, and current prices.
- Clear purchasing and support paths on small screens.
- Brand-owned presentation and source code.
- Provider-specific commerce stays out of presentation components.
